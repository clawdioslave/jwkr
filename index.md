# JWKR — Support

JWKR is a taste-driven trip planner for iPhone: answer 22 quick questions, name a city, and it writes each day like a chapter around what you love. No account, no server — your profile, trips, and private reviews stay on your device.

## Get help

Questions, bugs, or ideas: **clawdgross@gmail.com**

When reporting a bug, the iOS version and a screenshot help a lot.
