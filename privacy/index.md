# JWKR Privacy Policy

*Effective: August 20, 2026*

JWKR is built so that we could not read your data even if we wanted to — the app has
no server of its own, no accounts, and no analytics. It makes exactly two kinds of
outbound request, both described below: Apple's map services, and a public
opening-hours lookup. Neither carries anything that identifies you.

## What we collect

Nothing. JWKR collects no personal data, and we have no servers to collect it to.

## Opening hours

When you plan a trip, JWKR asks the public OpenStreetMap Overpass API for the
published opening hours of businesses in that city. That request contains a
geographic bounding box for the destination and nothing else — no name, no device
identifier, no profile, no indication of which specific places you're interested
in. It happens once per destination and is cached for the session. If it fails or
you're offline, the app falls back to its own category estimates and works
normally. Overpass is operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation under
[their privacy policy](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

## What stays on your device

Your taste profile (questionnaire answers and how they evolve), your trips and
routes, and your private post-visit reviews are stored only in the app's local
storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. They are included in your
own device backups according to your iOS settings.

## Location

If you grant location access, it is used once per request to center the map and
measure distances on your device. Location is never stored by JWKR and never leaves
your device through us. The app is fully functional without location access.

## Apple services

Maps, place search, geocoding, and directions are provided by Apple's MapKit and
Core Location system services. Those requests go to Apple and are governed by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). JWKR sends them no
identity information — it has none to send.

## Shared routes

Sharing a route creates a small file or link containing only place names,
coordinates, and suggested durations — no identity, no profile data, nothing about
you. It travels only through channels you choose (Messages, AirDrop, etc.).

## Private reviews

Reviews you write in JWKR are never published, uploaded, or visible to anyone else.
They exist solely to tune recommendations on your device.

## Children

JWKR does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children — it collects
no data at all.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example, adding an optional
sync feature), this policy will be updated first and the change clearly disclosed
in the App Store notes.
